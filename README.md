### Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce retail stall at Gikomba Market. While she manually records her daily sales transactions and tracks rough volumetric inventory stock by eye, she lacks visibility into data trends, cash flow velocity, and item profit margins. Without systematic tracking, she struggles to evaluate whether her store is scaling efficiently, faces optimization uncertainties surrounding high-volume foot traffic days, and risks structural stockouts or holding redundant capital in items with low margin profiles.

### Top Three Findings
1. **Healthy Business Growth Strategy:** Wanjiku's enterprise is scaling positively, experiencing a significant Week-on-Week revenue growth rate of **11.34%** from Week 1 (KES 22,050.00) to Week 2 (KES 24,550.00).
2. **Extreme Weekend Demand Dynamics:** Weekend performance drastically outshines weekdays; average sales pull in **KES 4,900.00** on Saturdays and Sundays compared to **KES 2,750.00** from Monday to Friday, representing an intensive weekend surge of **78.18%**.
3. **Imminent Stockouts on Key Pillars:** Critical high-margin assets are fundamentally understocked; **Tomatoes** (33.3% margin) and **Sukuma Wiki** (40.0% margin) have fallen below their threshold reorder benchmarks, risking immediate missed transaction opportunities.
